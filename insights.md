# 💡 Key Insights

A summary of the most important findings from this analysis.

---

## 1. Overall Revenue Growth
- Total company revenue grew by **$422,972.83** from 2023 to 2025
- This represents a **9.21% increase** from the 2023 baseline

---

## 2. Brand Performance Shifts

| Brand | 2023 Rank | 2025 Rank | Trend |
|-------|-----------|-----------|-------|
| FuelCore | #1 | #3 | ⬇️ Declined |
| RoastTrail | #2 | #5 | ⬇️ Declined |
| HomeNest | #4 | #1 | ⬆️ Rose significantly |

---

## 3. Marketing Spend Drives Revenue — But Channel Matters More

- A **strong positive correlation** exists between marketing spend and revenue across brands
- However, **raw spend alone is not the differentiator** — channel allocation is

| Brand | Wholesale Marketing % | Revenue Rank (2025) |
|-------|----------------------|---------------------|
| HomeNest | 29.5% | #1 |
| PureLife | 24.8% | Not top performer despite highest total spend |

---

## 4. Wholesale Is the Dominant Channel
- Wholesale averages **507 units sold** — far ahead of distributor (325) and other channels
- Brands that allocated more marketing budget to wholesale saw stronger revenue performance

---

## 5. The PureLife Anomaly
- PureLife had the **highest total marketing spend** of any brand
- Yet it did **not** rank as the top revenue generator
- Root cause: underallocation to the wholesale channel relative to competitors
- This suggests that **channel strategy is as important as spend volume**
